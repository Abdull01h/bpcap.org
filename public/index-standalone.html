Elevating Bangladesh's Baking & Pastry Artistry

Since 2019, BPCAB is the apex professional body uniting executive five-star pastry chefs, artisan sourdough bakers, cafe entrepreneurs, and home baking pioneers across Bangladesh.

Our Heritage

The Story & Purpose of BPCAB

Founded in 2019 in Dhaka, the Bakery and Pastry Chefs Assembly Of Bangladesh (BPCAB) was established to unify professional bakers, chocolatiers, and pastry innovators into a structured national guild.

🎯

Our Vision

To position Bangladesh as a recognized hub of pastry artistry and culinary craftsmanship in South Asia, where every pastry professional achieves dignity, skill certification, and global recognition.

⚡

Our Mission

To nurture homegrown talent through hands-on masterclasses, standardize hygiene & food safety across bakeries, and advocate for quality natural baking ingredients nationwide.

Executive Training

Masterclasses & Certification

Hands-on professional culinary bootcamps led by international guest masters and Bangladesh's top executive pastry chefs.

LEVEL 3 DIPLOMA

French Lamination & Viennoiserie

Croissant dough temperatures, butter folding techniques, pain au chocolat, kouign-amann, and advanced lamination mechanics.

Duration: 4 Weeks
MASTER ARTISTRY

Advanced Wedding Cake & Sugar Floristry

Structural tiered engineering, wafer paper flowers, delicate gum paste botanicals, and velvety smooth ganache contouring.

Duration: 2 Weeks
ARTISAN FERMENTATION

Wild Yeast Sourdough & Ancient Grains

Starter microbiology, baker's percentages, long cold autolyse, scoring patterns, and open-crumb hearth baking.

Duration: 3 Weeks
CHOCOLATIER

Couverture Tempering & Molded Bonbons

Cocoa butter spraying, crystallization curves, ganache emulsions, pâte de fruit, and high-gloss polycarbonate molding.

Duration: 2 Weeks
